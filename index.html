🔬 Why hearing loss isn’t “just turning the volume down” 🥁 Bass stays loud · 💬 Words blur
  • 1. Threshold loss: Quiet cymbal shimmer and soft consonants vanish completely.
  • 2. Loudness recruitment: Bass and drums still hit at full strength, so cranking volume booms.
  • 3. Frequency smearing: Widened cochlear filters blur s, f, th, k together in noisy pubs.
🛡️ Keep the music, skip the hangover −15 dB Clear Plugs · Quiet breaks
  • Filtered musicians’ earplugs: Lower sound by 15–20 dB evenly without muffling vocals.
  • Short chill-out breaks: 5–10 minutes away from main speakers lets hair cells recover.
  • Step back from PA stacks: Even 2 metres back cuts peak sound pressure sharply.

Why MSBG isn’t just turning the volume down
  • Threshold loss: soft high-frequency details fall below audibility and disappear.
  • Loudness recruitment: quiet detail vanishes while loud bass and drums hit at nearly full strength.
